Welcome

LiteNotebook can answer questions about your PDFs with an AI model that runs locally. This setup:

  1. checks your processor, memory, graphics card and free disk space;
  2. optionally installs MiniLM (about 90 MB) for semantic search;
  3. sets up llama.cpp — installed for you, one you already have, or an existing AI server;
  4. recommends a model that fits this computer and tests it.
