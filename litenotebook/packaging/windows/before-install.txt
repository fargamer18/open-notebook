LiteNotebook - local-first research notebook

LiteNotebook runs on this computer only. It opens in your web browser at
http://127.0.0.1 and listens to this computer only. It needs no account and
no internet connection for daily use.

What this installer does
- Installs the LiteNotebook program for your Windows user account only. No
  administrator rights are needed.
- Optionally installs the local semantic-search runtime (about 1 GB). It
  enables local embeddings and semantic search and is needed to add new
  PDFs. You can also add it later in Settings > Local AI.
- Does not download anything, and does not install any AI model, speech
  voice or notebook. Local AI components are offered later by LiteNotebook's
  own setup wizard, and are downloaded only when you choose them.
- Does not change which program opens PDF files.

Your data
- Notebooks, PDFs, notes, chats, backups, downloaded models and AI
  components are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LiteNotebook.
- Updating or uninstalling LiteNotebook never deletes that folder.

Licences and notices

- Bundled third-party components keep their own licences; see

This installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen
warning ("Windows protected your PC"). Choose "More info" and "Run anyway"
only if you downloaded it from the official LiteNotebook release page.
